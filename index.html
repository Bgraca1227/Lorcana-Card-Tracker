Screens

Each screen is a rule set for one kind of undervaluation. Tap a screen to open it in the screener; Tune edits its thresholds. Your rarity, set and filter choices apply on top.

Wait, don't buy yet

Cheap is not the same as done falling. These screens flag cards where the decline has not shown a floor.

Market

Where the whole tracked market sits relative to its own history. All figures are Near Mint, across every Enchanted, Iconic and promo card.

Set index

Median: the middle card's own move since the window start, which is what a typical card did. Value: the set's combined Near Mint price, which big cards dominate. Only cards with data for the full window count.

Settings

Live refresh

Pulls today's prices for every tracked card from JustTCG and merges them into the history stored on this phone. About 29 requests paced to the free tier (10 a minute), so it takes around three minutes. Keep the app open while it runs. The key never leaves this phone.

How to read it

Prices are Near Mint market prices for the foil printing (Enchanted and Iconic cards only exist as foils; promos use the foil where one exists). Lightly Played and other printings are on each card page.

Candles. The source records one price per day, so a daily candle opens at the previous close. Weekly candles are true open/high/low/close built from the daily prices.

Peak is the all-time high from the full sales record, or the post-launch high when the launch-spike setting is on and the all-time high fell inside the launch window.

Indicators run on daily closes. Card prices sit flat for days at a time, so treat RSI and Stoch RSI as a gauge of exhaustion, not a timing signal.

Not financial advice. A card that fell 70% can fall further. A screen is a shortlist to research, not a buy list.
